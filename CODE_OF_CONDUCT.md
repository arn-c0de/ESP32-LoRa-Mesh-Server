# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful community for all contributors and participants, regardless of background or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and inclusive in all interactions
- Welcome diverse perspectives and constructive criticism
- Focus discussions on the project and technical merits
- Thank contributors for their time and effort
- Help others learn and grow their skills

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated in this community:

- Harassment, discrimination, or hostile comments based on protected characteristics
- Intimidation or threats
- Spam or off-topic content
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Doxxing or sharing private information
- Deliberately disrupting discussions
- Any form of abuse or bullying

## Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. Document the incident (date, time, details)
2. Contact the project maintainer directly:
   - Email: arn-c0de@protonmail.com
3. Provide context and evidence if possible
4. Reports are handled confidentially

## Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:

- Warning from maintainers
- Temporary suspension from participation
- Permanent ban from the community (in severe cases)

Enforcement decisions will be made fairly and proportionally.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All project spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Official project events and channels
- Unofficial spaces where the project is discussed

## Acknowledgment

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, a widely-used community standard.

We value all contributors. Together, we create a respectful and productive community.
